# 🛍️ Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Retail Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI** to explore sales performance, profitability, product trends, and customer purchasing patterns.

The dashboard brings important business metrics together in one place, making it easier to monitor performance and understand how different products, categories, regions, and sales channels contribute to overall business results.

I designed this dashboard with a focus on clear data presentation, interactive filtering, and easy navigation so that users can explore the data and identify meaningful business insights.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard highlights the following business metrics:

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | 1.5M |
| Total Orders | 2K |
| Units Sold | 7K |
| Average Sales | 2.09K |
| Profit Margin | 0.36 |

It also includes a Sales vs Target indicator to help track overall sales performance against the target.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes several visualizations to analyze different aspects of retail performance.

- **Sales by Product:** Compares sales across different products to identify top-performing and lower-performing items.
- **Sales by Segment & Channel:** Shows how different customer segments and sales channels contribute to overall sales.
- **Profit by Category:** Compares profitability across different product categories.
- **City-wise Sales Analysis:** Uses a geographical map to explore sales distribution across cities.
- **Regional Sales vs Target:** Compares regional performance against sales targets.
- **Payment Method Distribution:** Shows the contribution of different payment methods to total sales.
- **Product Performance Analysis:** Compares product-level sales and profit performance.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers and filters to make data exploration easier.

- Region
- Payment Method
- Category
- Segment
- Sales Range

Users can apply these filters to explore specific business segments and analyze the results from different perspectives.
